## 3. The finding the accuracy number hides

| item | planted | blamed | shortlist contained it |
|---|---|---|---|
| airline-17-k4-tool_error | 4 | **4** | yes |
| retail-17-k10-tool_error | 10 | **10** | yes |
| retail-17-k4-tool_error | 4 | **4** | yes |
| airline-30-k8-tool_error | 8 | none | no |
| retail-106-k8-wrong_value | 8 | none | no |
| retail-29-k8-stale_record | 8 | none | no |

**Bisect is exact on 3 of 3 items whose shortlist contained the planted step, and blames
nothing on the 3 where it did not.** Accuracy decomposes as judge recall@3 (0.50) times a
conditional accuracy of 1.00. The confirmation step made no mistake in either direction:
no false blame, no miss when given the chance. **The shortlist is the entire loss.**

That is the risk `docs/brief/summary.md` §2 named in advance — "if the judge omits the
culprit, replay cannot find it" — and at this n it is the dominant term, not a footnote.

Worked example, `airline-17-k4-tool_error` (planted step 4):

| step | effect | 95% CI | treated | control | stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | **+0.75** | [0.358, 0.929] | 6/8 | 0/16 | blameworthy |
| 5 | 0.00 | [−0.194, 0.194] | 0/16 | 0/16 | max_n |
| 7 | 0.00 | [−0.194, 0.194] | 0/16 | 0/16 | max_n |

The judge's own answer was step **5**, which is wrong. Re-running its shortlist corrected it.

## 6. Why the test split is not completed

The agent model's serving capacity collapsed during the run and has not recovered.
Measured directly, with the evaluation stopped so we contributed no load:

| condition | result |
|---|---|
| 1 solo call | 2.4 s, succeeds |
| 4 concurrent | 4/4 succeed, 0.8–3.2 s |
| 10 concurrent, limiter 20 rpm | **1/10 succeed** in 328 s |
| 10 concurrent, limiter 40 rpm | **0/10 succeed** in 315 s |
| the evaluation at 12 in flight | **92% of calls retrying** |

P0 measured this model clean at 120 rpm (`config/limits.toml`), and the run itself sustained
110 calls/min at 17:30 the same day. The test split needs ~9,000 productive calls; at one
effective request in flight that is not reachable in any usable time. Nothing was published:
an incomplete pass writes no results, by design.

## 7. Engineering defects found before any number was published

Recorded because they are evidence about the process, and every one of them would have
produced a confident wrong answer:

1. the judge's output cap sat below its own chain-of-thought — **every item** would have
   scored as a parse failure and Bisect's accuracy would have been exactly 0;
2. the judge cache key omitted `max_tokens`, so the fix for (1) was served the truncated
   answer from cache;
3. a fork's live completion resolved to the replay dispatcher and **recursed** — every fork
   died, reported as a timeout;
4. infrastructure failures were scored as verdicts, publishing a network outage as 0.0
   accuracy for every method;
5. `rerun_id` omitted the prefix mode, so `rerun_live` silently **reused Bisect's forks** —
   the flaky comparison would have reported the two mechanisms as identical;
6. `no_control` was bought live instead of derived (0018 §2), a third of every evaluation;
7. killing a supervisor orphaned its child, so up to three evaluations shared one tape;
8. a retried fork's outcome was invisible to resume, so draws were re-bought for ever —
   control arms reached 85 recorded forks where the design needs 16.

Each is fixed with a regression test. (1), (3), (5) and (8) were only reachable on live
data, which is the argument for the dev split existing at all.
