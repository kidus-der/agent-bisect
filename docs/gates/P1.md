## Infrastructure failures

3 of the 20 runs first died on provider infrastructure (HTTP 504 gateway timeouts past our retry budget: 3 ledger rows with status `exhausted`) and were re-run. An infra failure is never scored as an agent failure: the attempt got no outcome row, stayed outstanding, and the resumed batch recorded it again under a fresh run id, because the tape is append-only and a retry may not overwrite the evidence of the attempt that crashed.
